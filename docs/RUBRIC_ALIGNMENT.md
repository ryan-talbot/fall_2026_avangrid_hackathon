# Challenge criteria and evidence

The organizer weights proposed solution and business value at 40%, analytics
and modeling at 35%, presentation and communication at 15%, and innovation
and creativity at 10%.

| Criterion | Evidence provided | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Utility relevance and actionability | Four customer program routes and a proposed shared-battery pilot. | Programs begin with customer confirmation and site review. |
| Battery opportunity assessment | Specific three-home alternative, common demand target, power/energy sizing and recharge limits. | The shared site requires physical network and metering assessment. |
| Quantified impact | 12.023 kW modeled peak relief and $9,250 lower assumed installed cost at 15%. | Tariffs and network costs are needed to establish bill savings or upgrade value. |
| Recommendation quality | Two headline targets, 40-target cost study, six additional groups and four-design replay. | Costs and the reduction target determine the preferred design. |
| Customer classification | Fixed meter/weather rules, household examples and held-out reference recovery. | EV recovery is 17/90 at 1,000 and heating recovery is 4/43 at 500. Unlisted status is unknown. |
| Segmentation | Four overlapping review routes across 33,674 meters, with evidence and proposed actions. | Review candidates require equipment and participation confirmation. |
| Technical rigor | Energy-conserving dispatch, household no-export limits, reserve, charging losses and 31 hand checks. | Historical optimization and chronological replay are labeled separately. |
| Use of supplied data | Hourly meters joined to weather and technology reference lists. | Complete-hour qualification and missing data are documented. |
| Reproducibility | Python source, fixed configurations, dependency pins, hashes and PowerShell commands. | Restricted inputs are supplied locally by authorized participants. |
| Communication | Shared-battery decision, annotated charts, program tables and a standalone presentation. | The presentation includes equations, assumptions and scope. |
| Innovation and additional insights | Customer offers connected to a fair home/shared comparison, target-dependent costs and charging timing. | Shared storage wins five extra group comparisons and ties one after refinement. |
| Scalability | Local processing of about 298 million input rows and 33,674 qualified profiles. | Production deployment and utility-system integration require further engineering. |

## Evidence boundaries

Appliance identification remains partial. Charger level, heating subtype,
water heaters, pools, hot tubs and battery ownership require additional labels
or confirmation. Known solar membership supports an export eligibility check.

Both smaller designs miss the July 1–4 cap after excluding those days from
sizing. Both larger designs pass after sizing with the full record. A future
field pilot tests equipment chosen before its operating period.
